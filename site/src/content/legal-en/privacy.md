<p>
  This policy describes the personal data collected by FerrLabs (Bryan Ferrando, sole
  proprietor, SIREN 104 243 951) acting as data controller for the marketing site
  <strong>ferrflow.com</strong>, in accordance with the GDPR.
</p>

<h2>Scope</h2>
<p>
  This policy covers ferrflow.com only. The FerrFlow CLI binary runs entirely on your machine
  and does not contact FerrLabs servers — no telemetry, no error reporting, no usage stats.
  Account, billing and team management on <code>app.ferrlabs.com</code> are governed by the
  <a href="https://ferrlabs.com/privacy">canonical FerrLabs privacy policy</a>.
</p>

<h2>Data collected</h2>
<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Server logs</strong>: IP address, user-agent, timestamps and requested URLs, kept
    for 30 days for security and abuse prevention.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>No cookies</strong>: ferrflow.com sets no cookie. The only client-side storage in
    use is the browser <code>localStorage</code> for remembering your language preference.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>No analytics</strong>: no audience tracker, no marketing pixel, no third-party
    script.
  </li>
</ul>

<h2>Purposes</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Operation and security of the marketing site.</li>
  <li>Compliance with legal obligations.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Legal bases (GDPR art. 6)</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Legitimate interest</strong> (6.1.f) for security and server logs.</li>
  <li><strong>Legal obligation</strong> (6.1.c) where applicable.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Subprocessors</h2>

</ul>

<h2>Your rights</h2>
<p>
  Under the GDPR, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability,
  objection and restriction of processing.
</p>
<p>
  To exercise your rights regarding the marketing site: <strong>contact&#64;ferrlabs.com</strong>.
  For account-related data rights (FerrLabs account on <code>app.ferrlabs.com</code>), please
  use the account dashboard or contact <strong>contact&#64;ferrlabs.com</strong>.
</p>
<p>
  You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (<a href="https://www.cnil.fr">cnil.fr</a>).
</p>

<h2>Data Protection Officer (DPO)</h2>
<p>
  FerrLabs has not appointed a DPO. This is not required for a sole proprietorship that does
  not carry out large-scale processing of sensitive data (GDPR art. 37).
</p>

<h2>Transfers outside the European Union</h2>
<p>
  No transfer of personal data is made outside the European Union for this site. Hosting is
  operated by OVH SAS in France.
</p>
